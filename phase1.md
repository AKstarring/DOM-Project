1. Business context
The tourism and hospitality industry involves multiple interconnected stakeholders, including hotels, travel operators, tourism authorities and local businesses. Their performance depends on factors such as tourist arrivals, seasonal demand, visitor spending, travel preferences and accommodation availability.
A major challenge in this industry is ensuring that tourism demand and hospitality capacity are aligned. Some destinations may experience high tourist arrivals and hotel occupancy, while others may have considerable accommodation capacity but relatively low demand. Similarly, a high number of tourists does not necessarily translate into proportionately higher tourism revenue, since spending patterns and purposes of travel can differ across destinations.
For businesses and tourism authorities, understanding these relationships is important when making decisions about hotel investments, marketing budgets, destination development and resource allocation.
The selected Tourism and Hospitality Industry Analysis Dataset provides an opportunity to examine these relationships across different geographical locations and time periods. Its documented variables include tourist arrivals, tourist spending, hotel occupancy, number of hotels, tourism revenue and contribution to GDP.
2. Business problem
The goal of this project is to optimize tourism Demand and Hospitality Resource Allocation Through Data Analytics
Tourism destinations and hospitality businesses face the challenge of aligning accommodation capacity and marketing investments with changing tourist demand. An imbalance between tourist arrivals and available hotel capacity can result in underutilized resources in some destinations and capacity constraints in others.
Furthermore, differences in tourist spending, purpose of visit and destination popularity mean that higher tourist arrivals do not necessarily translate into higher revenue.
The business problem is therefore to identify the factors influencing tourism demand and revenue generation, examine the relationship between tourist arrivals and hospitality capacity, and develop a predictive model to estimate tourist arrivals across destinations and time periods.
Using these insights, tourism authorities and hospitality businesses can make more informed decisions regarding marketing investments, capacity planning and destination development, ultimately improving resource utilization and tourism revenue.

6. Business objectives
The project aims to achieve the following objectives:
Identify tourism demand patterns across countries, cities and time periods.
Evaluate the relationship between tourist arrivals, tourist spending, hotel occupancy and tourism revenue.
Identify destinations where hospitality capacity may be underutilized or insufficient relative to demand.
Develop and evaluate a predictive model to estimate tourist arrivals based on relevant destination and time-related characteristics.
Translate analytical findings into actionable recommendations for tourism marketing, hospitality capacity planning and investment decisions.
